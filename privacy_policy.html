Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At Homehub we care about your privacy. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it and how we keep it safe.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password when you sign up.
  • Contact messages: your name, email and message when you use our contact form.
  • Projects you share: photos, descriptions and comments you post on Homehub.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your Homehub account.
  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To show your projects to the Homehub community.

We will never sell your information to anyone.

3. How we store your information

At the moment this website is a student project. Sign up details are only saved in your own web browser (local storage) and are not sent to a server. You can remove them at any time by clearing your browser data.

4. Cookies

Homehub does not use tracking or advertising cookies.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to see, change or delete the information we hold about you at any time.

6. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, please visit our Contact page or email us at info@homehub.com.
