Safety & Accessibility

How to stay safe when doing DIY, and how we make Homehub easy for everyone to use.

Important: Never do your own gas work, and only do electrical work if you are qualified. Always use a Gas Safe registered engineer or a qualified electrician.

DIY Safety Tips

Wear protective gear

Goggles, gloves and a dust mask protect your eyes, hands and lungs. Wear strong shoes when moving heavy things.

Use power tools safely

Read the instructions first. Unplug tools before changing blades or bits, and keep your fingers away from the cutting edge.

Ladders and working high up

Put the ladder on flat, firm ground and never stand on the top step. If you can, have someone hold the bottom.

Check before you drill

Use a pipe and cable detector before drilling into walls or floors, so you do not hit a water pipe or a live wire.

Paint, glue and fresh air

Open windows when using paint, glue or sprays. Take breaks outside if you feel dizzy or get a headache.

Lift safely and get help

Bend your knees and keep your back straight when lifting. Ask a friend to help with anything big or heavy.

Every project on Homehub also has its own ⚠️ Stay safe box with tips for that job. Browse the projects.

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use Homehub. Here is what we do:

  • Image descriptions: photos have alternative text, so screen readers can describe them to blind users.
  • Works on any screen: pages change their layout for phones, tablets and computers.
  • Labelled forms: every box in our forms has a label that says what to type in it.
  • Labelled menu button: on phones, screen readers read the menu button out as "Toggle menu".
  • Clear headings: pages use headings in order, so they are easy to scan and to move around with a screen reader.
  • Zoom friendly: you can zoom in with your browser (Ctrl and +) and the page will still fit.
